Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how mobile games and applications published by Nofil Bashir (“we”, “us”, or the “Developer”) handle information. It applies to all of our apps available on Google Play (each, the “App”). By using an App, you agree to the practices described here.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location from within our Apps. Our Apps are casual word and puzzle games that can be played offline; advertising, optional in-app purchases, and notifications need a network connection. Some limited information is collected automatically by the third-party services described below, primarily to display ads, deliver purchases, and understand basic usage.

Information collected automatically

When you use an App, the third-party services we integrate may collect:

  • A device advertising identifier (e.g. Google Advertising ID).
  • Approximate (non-precise) location derived from your IP address.
  • Device information such as model, operating-system version, and language.
  • App usage and diagnostic events (for example, levels started or completed, and crash data).
  • Which in-app item you bought, if you make a purchase (for example, a coin pack). Your payment details are handled entirely by Google Play and are never seen by us.
  • A notification token, if you allow notifications, so that reminders and occasional news about our Apps can be delivered to your device.

This information is processed by the third parties on their own infrastructure. We use it to understand overall usage, to diagnose crashes, to deliver what you have bought, and to show and measure ads. We do not use it to work out who you are.

Third-party services we use

  • Google AdMob — serves the advertisements shown in our Apps and may use the advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — provides anonymous, aggregated usage and stability statistics so we can improve our Apps. See the Firebase Privacy and Security information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — records crash reports and diagnostic information (such as device model, operating-system version, and the point in the code where the App stopped working) so we can find and fix faults.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers notifications to your device, if you have allowed them.
  • Firebase Remote Config — lets us adjust settings inside an App (such as how often ads appear) without publishing an update.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases. Google Play handles your payment; we receive only confirmation of what was bought.

These services are operated by Google LLC and are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

How information is used

  • To display and measure advertising (including, where permitted, personalized ads).
  • To understand which features are used and to fix crashes and bugs.
  • To deliver items you have bought and to see which products players find useful.
  • To send reminders and occasional news about our Apps, if you have allowed notifications.
  • To keep our Apps secure and working reliably.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Where required by law, we request your consent for personalized advertising the first time you open an App, and you can change that choice from within the App.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time, either in the App’s settings or in your device settings.
  • You can remove everything an App has stored on your device by uninstalling it. We hold no account for you, so there is nothing further for us to delete.

Children’s privacy

Our Apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by the third-party services above is retained according to their respective policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
