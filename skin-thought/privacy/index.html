Skin Thought Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Skin Thought is a skincare companion app published by Dot and Round Studio, operated by Nofil Bashir ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your shelf, routines and label photos stay on your phone. We collect only anonymous usage and crash data to keep the app working well.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into the app is stored only on your device. We never receive it:

  • The name you choose to be greeted by (optional), your skin type and the concerns you pick.
  • The products on your shelf, their ingredient lists, colours and favourites.
  • Your routines, reminder times, streaks and the clashes you marked as okay.
  • The topics and ingredients you save or have read, and your recent searches.

Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had a copy.

Camera and photos

When you scan a label, the photo is read on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The ingredient list is matched and checked for clashes on your phone too. The photo is deleted as soon as it has been read. Photos are never uploaded, never sent to us or to analytics, and never stored in your gallery by the app. If you pick a photo from your gallery instead, the app reads a temporary copy and deletes that copy; your original photo is not changed.

The app asks for camera access only when you open the scanner. It does not use the microphone.

Information collected automatically

To understand how the app is used and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase services. They may collect:

  • Usage events, for example "a label was scanned", "a routine was marked done", "a topic was opened". Events carry counts and categories (such as how many ingredients were found or which topic was opened), never your product names, your name, ingredient text or photos.
  • Crash and error reports: which part of the app failed, the device model, Android version and app version.
  • Device information such as model, Android version, language, and an approximate country or region derived from your IP address.
  • App instance identifiers created by Firebase for this installation, used to count users and group crash reports. They are not linked to your name or account (the app has no accounts).

The app does not collect your advertising ID, precise location, contacts, or any health records.

Notifications

Routine reminders are scheduled on your phone and work without the internet. If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging gives the app a notification token so we can occasionally send general announcements about the app to everyone. We do not send messages based on your skin, products or routines, because we do not know them.

Sharing

If you share your shelf, a routine or a topic, the app creates a picture on your phone and opens Android's share menu. Where it goes is your choice; we do not see it.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging), operated by Google. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your device. See ML Kit terms.

The app shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Usage and crash data held by Firebase is kept according to Google's retention settings for those services and is used only in aggregate.

Educational, not medical

Skin Thought explains ingredients and common skincare questions for learning only. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a medical device. For a skin condition, or before using prescription products, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • You can deny camera access and add products by typing the ingredient list instead.
  • You can turn off notifications in Android settings at any time.
  • You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it or clearing its storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
